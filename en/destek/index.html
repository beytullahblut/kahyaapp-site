I changed phones

On the old phone: Settings → Back up and save the file (Drive, iCloud, e-mail). On the new phone: Restore. For Plus, tap Restore purchases on the Plus screen.

How do I add a second car?

The free version tracks one vehicle. Unlimited vehicles come with Kahya Plus.

Fuel consumption shows "—"

Enter the odometer reading with each fill-up; consumption is calculated between two fill-ups.

Cancel Plus

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
