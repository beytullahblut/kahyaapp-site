Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Kahya has no server and needs no account. You can create a backup in Settings and save it wherever you like.

What if I change phones?

Create a backup on the old phone and restore it on the new one. Plus is tied to your store account; use Restore Purchases.

Does it work with electric cars?

Yes. Charging is recorded in kWh; hybrids can log both fuel and charging.

Can it read receipts?

For now records are entered by hand; the most used fields are suggested from your last entry, so a fill-up takes seconds. Photo reading is coming in a later version.

If I cancel Plus, do I lose my records?

No. All records stay; only the Plus screens are locked.
